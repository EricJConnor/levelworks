…isms — as a tag

Built from the beads and the bubble, but shaped like something you sew into a collar or stitch on a hat.

1 · bubble badge
The bubble as a solid dark shape with the three beads inside. Reads as a patch. Two thread colors plus the beads.
2 · round patch
Beads above, word below, a ring around it. Hat front, sleeve, sticker. Embroiders in four colors.
3 · woven label
The actual neck label: stitched border, beads, word, the line underneath. This is what a buyer sees when they pull the collar.
4 · stacked
Beads on top, word underneath. A square mark, which is what app icons, profile pictures and hang tags want.
5 · bubble, beads only
No word at all. The typing bubble with the three beads is the icon; "isms" sits next to it in text wherever there's room. This is the one that works at 16 pixels.
6 · icon plus wordmark
The bead bubble as the icon, the word beside it. Website header, email signature, business card. Icon and word can also split up.
