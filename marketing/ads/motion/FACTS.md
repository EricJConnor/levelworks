## What the app does (all live)
- Write an estimate on your phone. Line items with quantity and rate, a running total.
- Send it to the client by email, text (from the contractor's own phone) or link.
- The client opens it on their phone, approves and signs it.
- Convert the estimate to an invoice in one tap.
- The client pays the invoice link by card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover,
  Apple Pay, Google Pay) or by bank transfer.
- The money goes to the contractor's own bank through Stripe. LevelWorks takes no fee on
  payments; only Stripe's standard fees apply (card 2.9% + 30c; bank 0.8% capped at $5).
- Payment timing (be honest): the client's payment is instant; Stripe sends it to the
  bank on the account's payout schedule, about two business days for a card.
  Never say "immediately" or "same day" about the bank.
- Unlimited estimates. Unlimited invoices. Recurring billing.
- The whole app in English and Spanish. Line items the contractor writes are translated
  in one tap (Traducir al inglés / Translate to Spanish), reviewed side by side, prices locked.
- Photo job updates, deposits on estimates, balances on invoices, receipts.
- Built by a contractor (Eric Connor, EC Home Improvement).
