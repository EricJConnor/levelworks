# LevelWorks Facebook groups tracker

Its own file, never mixed with the isms sheet. Built from Muse's research of Oct 28 2026 (98
groups Eric is already in, 310 new) and updated from Cowork's end-of-day tables. Muse's full
tables live in her `levelworks_tracker.xlsx` / the two CSVs; this file holds the working lists.
Group code = `utm_campaign` for that group, assigned once, never reused.

## Rules of the routine (fixed)

10 posts and 20 joins a day, 20 minutes between posts, stop at 9 pm ET, read each group's rules
before every post, rotate the four post versions, reply to every comment the same day, any
Facebook warning stops the day. Nothing new posts without Eric's go the first time.

## The one thing Muse flagged that changes everything

Most "promo allowed" groups mean contractors may advertise **to homeowners**. LevelWorks is a
pitch **to contractors**, which several groups ban by name (software, estimating services,
vendors, marketers). So the lane is: groups where contractors talk business with each other,
groups that welcome vendors or business posts outright, and busy job boards with no rules. The
post reads as one contractor showing another what he built, which is true, and that is the only
reason it belongs in any of them.
